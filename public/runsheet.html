Recording run-sheet

Open this on your phone and prop it next to the laptop. Tick as you go. Leave two seconds of silence at the start and end of every clip.

Before you start
Stop a recording / if QuickTime disappears

Click the stop icon in the top menu bar, or press Cmd+Ctrl+Esc. The file lands on your Desktop.

Clip A · Hook · about 40 s

Landing page

Narration 01-hook and 02-what are added later. You only hold the screen.

Clip B · Live capture · about 90 s

Pip and you, no narration

This is the heart of the video. Speak naturally, like a tired expert. If you fumble, stop and retake the whole clip.

Clip C · Map and tutor · about 90 s

Same window as B

Narration 03-map and 04-tutor go over the first 30 seconds. Then Pip's live voice takes over.

Clip D · Close · about 35 s

New window

Narration 05-principles starts about 8 seconds in, after Pip's greeting. 06-close goes over the landing page.

Afterwards
If something goes wrong

Pip never asks: you clicked too fast. Wait 3 s after the decision, or click Next.

Pip talks over you: stop, wait for "Watching quietly", retake that clip.

No sound from Pip in the recording: raise the volume to 60% and check the Microphone choice in Cmd+Shift+5 → Options.

"Connection problem" banner: reload the page. If it persists, check the agent ids in the Vercel settings.
